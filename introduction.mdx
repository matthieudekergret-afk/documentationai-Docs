## Welcome to Matthieu de KERGRET

This starter kit gives you a clean place to organize product knowledge, internal notes, and user-facing documentation. You can adapt every page to match your workflow, while keeping the structure simple enough for readers to scan quickly.

Use this space as your homepage, your map, and your first touchpoint for new users. It is designed to help you present information clearly, route people to the right section, and keep content easy to maintain as your project grows.
