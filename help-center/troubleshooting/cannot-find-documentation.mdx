---
title: Cannot Find Documentation
description: What to do if documentation is missing or hard to locate.
---

## Symptoms
- Missing project docs
- Broken documentation links
- Difficulty searching content

## Quick Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check documentation links">
    Confirm you are on `https://matthieu-docs.com` or your assigned space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use search feature">
    Try searching document titles or keywords from the top bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact admin">
    Email `support@matthieu-docs.com` for missing or restricted content.
  </Step>
</Steps>
