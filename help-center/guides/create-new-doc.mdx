## Quick Start

<Steps>
  <Step title="Login">
    Go to `https://matthieu-docs.com` and sign in to your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start new doc">
    Click on "New Document" or the "+" icon in your project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in content">
    Enter a title, description, and main text for your documentation page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and publish">
    Hit "Save" to draft, or "Publish" to make it live for your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
